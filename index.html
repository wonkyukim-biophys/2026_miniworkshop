About the workshop

Bio-soft matter physics sits at the meeting point of statistical physics, soft condensed matter, and the living world — where entropic elasticity, thermal fluctuations, and non-equilibrium activity shape membranes, biopolymers, and cellular machinery. This informal, single-day workshop brings together a small group of researchers to share recent work and open questions at these frontiers.

The day is built around five invited talks with generous time for discussion and an afternoon poster session. There are no parallel sessions — the aim is a focused, conversational atmosphere.
